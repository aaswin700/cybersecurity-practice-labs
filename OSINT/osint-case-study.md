# OSINT Practice Case Study

## Objective
To understand how information available on the internet can be collected
and analyzed for learning purposes.

## Scope
This exercise was done using dummy data and publicly available information.
No real individual or private account was targeted.

## Method Used
- Basic search engine queries
- Username search across platforms
- Observing publicly shared information

## Observations
- Same usernames can appear on multiple platforms
- Small pieces of information can be linked together
- Oversharing online increases privacy risks

## Learning
This activity helped me understand the importance of online privacy
and how OSINT is useful in cybersecurity investigations.
